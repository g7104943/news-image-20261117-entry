## Main stories (the four panels)

### 1. UKRAINE — Bridge war: Crimea link hit, Kyiv bridges struck
- Ukraine struck the Kerch (Crimean) Bridge early on Tuesday, the second
  attack in less than 24 hours; the Moscow-installed head of Crimea,
  Sergei Aksyonov, said traffic was suspended. President Zelenskyy hailed
  "an unprecedented operation" weeks in the making.
- Overnight, Russian ballistic missiles damaged two of Kyiv's bridges over
  the Dnipro (North and South bridges), disrupting transport, heating,
  water and power.
- Sources: Al Jazeera / Outlook India / The War Zone (via Algenom daily
  briefing, 17 Nov 2026); Just Security Early Edition, 17 Nov 2026;
  Nhan Dan world brief, 17 Nov 2026; Kyiv mayor Vitali Klitschko.

### 2. MIDDLE EAST — Hormuz tanker attacks; third US carrier arrives
- Iran's Revolutionary Guard navy said two oil tankers struck mines and
  caught fire on a route south of the Strait of Hormuz; Iran's regular
  navy said further tankers were hit east of the strait.
- Iran's IRGC said it fired drones at a US military radar system at the
  Sheikh Isa base in Bahrain; Bahrain said it intercepted the attack, with
  no casualties or damage reported.
- The USS Theodore Roosevelt carrier strike group arrived in the Middle
  East, bringing the number of US carrier strike groups in the region to
  three — the first three-carrier concentration there since April.
- Sources: Al Jazeera / Reuters (via Arab News, Algenom, 17 Nov 2026);
  Just Security Early Edition, 17 Nov 2026; Nhan Dan, 17 Nov 2026;
  Zark News daily briefing, 17 Nov 2026.

### 3. DIPLOMACY — First Xi–Trump–Putin summit set for Thursday in Shenzhen
- China confirmed that Presidents Xi Jinping, Donald Trump and Vladimir
  Putin will hold their first trilateral summit on Thursday 19 November
  in Shenzhen, on the sidelines of the APEC leaders' meeting (18–19 Nov).
  Foreign ministry spokeswoman Mao Ning called it the first face-to-face
  meeting of the three countries' heads of state; no agenda was given.
  (Confirmed by Beijing on Monday 16 Nov; carried worldwide on 17 Nov.)
- The White House had said Trump will raise nuclear-arms cuts and Iran;
  the New START treaty expired on 5 February 2026.
- Sources: China's Foreign Ministry / Xinhua (via Korea Times, Manila
  Times, Economic Times, Outlook India, 16–17 Nov 2026); The Korea Times,
  16 Nov 2026; Manila Times, 17 Nov 2026.

### 4. TECHNOLOGY / ECONOMY — A wave of outages hits banks, the FAA, Microsoft
- A string of high-profile tech outages dominated the day: JPMorgan Chase
  systems were disrupted nationwide (the bank said transactions were back
  to normal during the day); Microsoft services, an FAA router-induced
  ground stop, Apple services and an NSA internal system were also hit.
- Cybersecurity experts said the outages were typically not cyberattacks,
  but showed how interconnected systems have become.
- Source: Reuters, "Multiple tech systems have been crashing. Here is how
  to protect yourself", 17 Nov 2026; Outlook India live blog, 17 Nov 2026
  (JPMorgan transactions back to normal).
